## Meet the People area

The **People** area is your contact database inside Tented. Every person you market to — leads from tent forms, imported lists, customers you add by hand — lives here, ready to be organized into lists and reached through [email blasts](../email/email-blasts) and [triggered flows](../email/triggered-flows).

Click **People** in the sidebar to see everyone in your database. You can sort by any column, search by name or email, and customize which columns appear with the **Columns** button.

![A screenshot of the All People tab showing a table of contacts with Display Name, Email, Phone, Company, and Updated columns.](../images/people-all-people.png)

## Adding contacts

Click the **Add** button in the top-right corner to see your options:

![A screenshot of the Add menu with options to Add Contact, Import from CSV, Create Dynamic List, and Create Static List.](../images/people-add-menu.png)

### Quick Add

Perfect for adding one person at a time. An email *or* phone number is required — everything else is optional:

- **Email** and **Phone**
- **First Name** and **Last Name**
- **Company** and **Job Title**

![A screenshot of the Quick Add form filled in with a contact named Maya Chen from Acme Robotics.](../images/people-quick-add.png)

Click **Create** and the contact appears in your database instantly.

### Import from CSV

Got a whole list? Choose **Import from CSV** (or the **Import CSV** tab in the Add People dialog) and drag in your file. Tented reads your column headers and auto-maps them to contact fields — you can adjust any mapping and choose whether imported values **overwrite** fields that are already filled.

![A screenshot of the Configure Import screen showing CSV columns auto-mapped to Tented fields with overwrite options.](../images/people-import-csv-mapping.png)

Click **Start Import** and watch the progress in real time. When it's done you get a row-by-row breakdown of what was created, updated, skipped, or failed — and you can export the results as a CSV for your records.

![A screenshot of a completed import showing 6 rows created with a detailed row results table.](../images/people-import-csv-complete.png)

<Tip>
  Past imports stick around under the **Past Imports** tab, so you can always go back and review what happened in a previous upload.
</Tip>

## Viewing a contact

Click any contact's name to open their profile. The **Details** tab shows all their standard fields — name, email, company, job title, address, lead status, lifecycle stage, and their **Unsubscribed** status for email marketing. You'll also see which [lists](/people/working-with-lists) they belong to and any [triggered flows](../email/triggered-flows) they're enrolled in.

![A screenshot of a contact's profile showing standard fields like name, email, company, job title, and email domain.](../images/people-contact-details.png)

The **Activities** tab is a running timeline of everything that's happened with this contact — when they were created, which fields were updated, forms they submitted, and lists they joined or left.

![A screenshot of a contact's Activities tab showing a Person Created event with its source and filled fields.](../images/people-contact-activities.png)

## Filtering people

Click **Filters** to slice your database with audience rules. Rules can match on:

- **Contact property** — any standard field, like *Job Title contains "Marketing"*
- **Activity** — things that happened, like *Form Submitted in the last 7 days*
- **List membership** — whether someone is (or isn't) on a specific list

Combine multiple rules and groups to get as specific as you need.

![A screenshot of the Filter People dialog showing the audience rule builder.](../images/people-filters.png)

<Note>
  These are the same audience rules used to build [dynamic lists](/people/working-with-lists#dynamic-lists) — so if you find yourself applying the same filter over and over, save it as a dynamic list instead.
</Note>

## Bulk actions

Select one or more contacts with the checkboxes and the **People Actions** menu lights up. From there you can:

- **Edit** contact fields
- **Add to List** — drop them into an existing static list or create a new one on the spot
- **Export as CSV**
- **Delete** contacts you no longer need

![A screenshot of the People Actions menu with Edit, Add to List, Export as CSV, and Delete options.](../images/people-actions-menu.png)
