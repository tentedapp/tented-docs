## Inviting Users Overview

Tented workspaces are built for teams — invite as many teammates as you need (every plan includes unlimited users). Admins handle invitations from **Settings > Team**.

## Sending an Invitation

1. Click your **profile** in the bottom-left corner and select **Settings**.
2. Choose **Team** in the settings menu.
3. Click **Invite New User**.

<img src="/images/team-management.png" alt="A screenshot of the Team section of Settings with the All Users table and the Invite New User button." width="832" />

4. Fill in the invitation:
   - **Full name** — how they'll appear in the workspace
   - **Email address** — where the invitation is sent
   - **Role** — **Standard User** or **Admin** (see [User Permissions](/configuring-tented/user-permissions))
5. Click **Send invite**.

<img src="/images/invite-new-user.png" alt="A screenshot of the Invite New User dialog with full name, email address, and role fields." width="508" />

Your teammate receives an email with a **magic link** — no password setup required. One click and they're in.

## After the Invite

New users appear in the **All Users** table with their role and status. From there you can [manage them](/configuring-tented/managing-users) — change their role with **Edit User** in the row's **⋯** menu.

<Tip>
  Not sure which role to pick? Default to **Standard User** — you can always promote someone to **Admin** later.
</Tip>
