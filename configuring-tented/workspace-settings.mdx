## Workspace Settings Overview

Workspace settings control your company information, branding, team, integrations, and Tented plan: everything shared by your whole workspace.

Click your **profile** in the bottom-left corner, then select **Settings** from the menu that opens.

<img src="/images/workspace-settings-access.png" alt="A screenshot of the profile menu with the Settings item highlighted." width="254" />

Settings opens on the **Workspace** section. The menu on the left switches between sections:

- **Workspace**: workspace details and company information (below).
- **Brand**: your logo, icon, color, and brand guidelines (below).
- **Team**: invite and manage teammates. See [Managing Users](/configuring-tented/managing-users).
- **Plans and usage**: your plan, what you've used this period, and the plans you can move to. See [Managing Your Subscription](/billing-subscriptions/managing-subscription).
- **Integrations**: connect Slack, Microsoft Teams, HubSpot, Clay, and more.
- **Email**: sending domain, sender profile, default headers, and unsubscribe footer. See [Setting Up Email](/email/email-setup).
- **People**: [standard and custom contact fields](/people/contact-fields).
- **API**: API keys and usage. See [API Keys & Usage](/configuring-tented/api-keys).
- **User Settings**: your own profile. See [User Settings](/configuring-tented/user-settings).

<img src="/images/workspace-settings.png" alt="A screenshot of the Settings page on the Workspace section, showing Workspace Details and Company Information." width="1470" />

## Workspace Details

- **Workspace Name**: The internal name of your workspace, used in system notifications like user invites.
- **Workspace Slug**: A unique identifier used as the prefix for your published tents (e.g., `t8rvby15uq.tented-pages.com/...`). Contact support to request a change.
- **Branded Domain**: The CNAME alias from your custom domain for published tents. See [Custom Domains](/configuring-tented/custom-domains).

## Company Information

Give Tented context for on-brand AI generation:

- **Company Name** and **Website**
- **Company Description**: A brief description of what your company does.

## Brand

- **Primary Brand Color**: Your main brand color (used for buttons and accents).
- **Brand Logo**: PNG, JPG, or SVG with a 3:1 aspect ratio and transparent background (max 2MB).
- **Brand Icon**: PNG, SVG, or ICO with a 1:1 (square) aspect ratio (max 1MB).
- **Brand Guidelines**: Free-text guidance the AI follows when generating content — fonts to prefer, tone, things to avoid.

Find these under **Settings > Brand**.
