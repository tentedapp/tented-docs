## Team Overview

Everything about your team lives in **Settings > Team**: click your **profile** in the bottom-left corner, select **Settings**, then choose **Team**.

<img src="/images/team-management.png" alt="A screenshot of the Team section of Settings showing the All Users table with name, email, role, status, created date, and a row actions menu." width="832" />

## The All Users Table

For each member you'll see:

- **Full Name** and **Email Address**
- **Role** — **Admin** or **Standard User** (see [User Permissions](/configuring-tented/user-permissions))
- **Status** — **Active**, or **Pending** until they accept their invitation
- **Created Date** — when they joined
- **Actions** — the **⋯** menu at the end of the row, with **Edit User** and **Delete User**

## Common Tasks

- **Add someone** — click **Invite New User** (full guide: [Inviting New Users](/configuring-tented/inviting-new-users))
- **Change a role** — choose **⋯ > Edit User** on their row and pick the new role
- **Remove someone** — choose **⋯ > Delete User**. They lose access right away, and the content they created stays
- **Check your seat count** — the total is shown next to the **All Users** heading; every Tented plan includes unlimited users, so invite freely

<Note>
  Only **Admins** can send invitations, change roles, and remove users.
</Note>
